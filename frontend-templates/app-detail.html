About this app

WhatsApp from Meta is a FREE messaging and video calling app. It's used by over 2B people in more than 180 countries. It's simple, reliable, and private, so you can easily keep in touch with your friends and family.

Features:

  • MESSAGE PRIVATELY - End-to-end encryption keeps your personal messages between you and the people you choose.
  • SIMPLE AND RELIABLE - Messages are delivered quickly, even on slow connections.
  • FREE CALLS - Make free voice and video calls to friends and family.
  • GROUP CHAT - Enjoy group chats with your contacts so you can easily stay in touch.
  • SHARE MOMENTS - Send photos, videos, and documents without limits.
  • VOICE MESSAGES - Say what's on your mind with just a tap.
